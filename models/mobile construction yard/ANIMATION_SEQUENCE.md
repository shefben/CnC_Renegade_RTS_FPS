# Animation sequence

Timeline: 0-180 frames

## 0-30
- Vehicle sits in travel configuration.

## 30-70
- Track pods spread outward.
- Main floor lowers.
- Front industrial platform begins shifting.

## 70-120
- Roof wings rotate outward and downward into the pitched Construction Yard roof.
- Central deck expands and rises.
- Hidden construction-yard walls, floor, and garage framing begin to deploy.

## 120-150
- Garage door frame and slats appear.
- Concrete corner buttresses rise.
- Crane base and mast emerge.

## 150-180
- Crane arm extends and claw drops.
- Catwalk rails, ridge cap, roof vents, and lighting settle.
- Final Construction Yard stance locks in.

The animation is a rigid-body mechanical deployment sequence.
No skin or vertex-morph animation is required.
