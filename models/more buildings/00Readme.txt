This zip file contains all of the raw assets that we used to create the buildings in Renegade.  Both the multiplayer and single player buildings are contained in this zip file with their interiors.  In some cases you will find variations of the multiplayer buildings that had underground rooms which we ended up cutting.  As you may notice, some buildings are very complex and are made of many different models.  Usually they are set up so that when you instantiate the model for the exterior of the building, it proxies in all of the other needed models in the proper places.  This is advanced stuff and may contain old assets that are missing textures; there's just too much for me to manually verify everything.  Have fun!

Directories:

BuildingSourceArt - All of the raw assets that I could find that were used to create our single and multiplayer buildings.  Interiors are included whereever possible.

C&CMapTemplates - The template for creating a flying and non-flying C&C mode map. These have actually already been released before but we put them here too.
